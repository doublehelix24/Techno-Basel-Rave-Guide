01 / SATURDAY 24 OCTOBER 2026 / GROUP FIT 7/10

Techno to Trance

ALT8 · Kichta · ROW1 · Nella · Dekerim

VENUE MÄX · Hardstrasse 219, 8005 Zurich
HOURS 23:00-approximately 06:00 · 18+ · Closing the following morning
PRESENTER MÄX / Techno to Trance
LINE-UP ALT8, Kichta, ROW1, Nella and Dekerim. Nella is described as opening and Dekerim as closing; individual set times are not published.
TICKETS Phase I CHF 27; Phase II CHF 32. Early Bird CHF 22 unavailable. No event-specific VIP ticket price verified. Buy tickets

The sound and the artists

This is the fastest, most playful rave in the selection: expect roughly 145-160+ BPM, springy offbeat bass, forceful kicks, acid sounds and bright Trance melodies. Familiar vocal fragments can appear inside much tougher club arrangements.

ALT8, an Irish producer and GOTEC resident, combines early-2000s dance nostalgia with Techno drums and euphoric Trance. His collaborations with Sara Landry show the harder side of his catalogue.

Kichta, the French duo, mix Techno, Trance, '90s House and acid with a rougher, cheekier edge. ROW1 adds hard-dance momentum; Nella and Dekerim reinforce the bouncy, uplifting direction.

Expected experience: An energetic club rave with more bouncing and rapid-fire excitement than slow, cinematic build-ups. Melody lovers may enjoy it enormously, but melody here comes with considerable speed and impact.

The sound and the artists

Expect approximately 126-132 BPM, rolling bass, tightly layered percussion and long stretches of uninterrupted groove. Small changes accumulate: a new hi-hat, a bassline shift, a vocal fragment appearing at exactly the right moment.

Marco Carola, the artist behind Music On, is a major reference for Ibiza's groove-led club culture. Loco Dice brings a muscular, rhythm-focused approach with roots in Hip-Hop. Seth Troxler, raised around Detroit's musical culture, adds characterful House selections and a less predictable touch.

Fleur Shore provides another reason to arrive early: her productions offer punchy, direct House grooves.

Expected experience: An extended Ibiza-style dancefloor session inside a large industrial venue. Strong for people who enjoy dancing continuously; less obvious for anyone chiefly seeking soaring melodies and singalong choruses.

The sound and the artists

The most Techno-focused Warehouse line-up: expect roughly 135-145+ BPM during harder sections, dense bass, acid sequences and hypnotic repetition.

Charlotte de Witte is a global Techno headliner whose sets combine forceful drums, tension and acidic or Trance-influenced sounds. Recent selections also show psychedelic textures; this is broader than an uninterrupted stream of industrial kicks.

FJAAK add a rawer, more physical rhythmic character. Adiel provides a deeper, more hypnotic counterweight. The result should offer more variety within Techno than the headliner alone might suggest.

Expected experience: Sustained physical intensity, darker immersion and fewer conventional singalong moments. Strong for dedicated Techno fans; a bigger stylistic jump for someone coming mainly for vocal House.

The sound and the artists

The clearest choice for big synthesizer melodies with a substantial Techno foundation. Expect approximately 124-132 BPM, deep bass, extended breakdowns and dramatic releases.

ARTBAT, the Ukrainian duo behind UPPERGROUND, favour a powerful, expansive sound: ominous basslines, huge melodic phrases and emotionally charged vocal moments. Their international profile bridges specialist melodic events and major festival stages.

Miss Monique brings a more flowing connection between Progressive House and Melodic Techno. Her long-form mixes reference gradual progression, although her mainstage sets can be more immediate and forceful.

Expected experience: Emotional tension followed by collective release. More cinematic than HUGEL, more melody-led than Charlotte de Witte, and generally less dependent on familiar choruses than Axwell.

The sound and the artists

The richest Progressive House-Melodic Techno bridge in the programme. Expect much of the music around 122-130 BPM, with darker, more forceful passages possible.

Eric Prydz builds long arcs through repeating motifs, careful layering and patient tension. His Pryda and Cirez D catalogues range from luminous Progressive House to darker club pressure. Recent sets contain unreleased IDs and private edits alongside recognizable productions.

Mind Against, Italian-born duo Alessandro and Federico Fognini, connect House and Techno with atmospheric electronics. Innellea adds cinematic melancholy, rougher sound design and restless drama. Yulia Niko provides a groove-led melodic bridge.

Expected experience: A night that rewards arriving early and following the changing mood. Strongest overall for melody, depth and enough rhythmic force to satisfy House and Techno interests. The booking does not confirm a HOLO show or another named Prydz production.

GROOVE, EMOTION AND PROGRESSION

How the musical journeys differ

Carola / Loco Dice / Troxler vs. HUGEL

Both are rhythm-led House options, but they reward different listening habits.

The opening emphasizes the craft of the mix: long blends, detailed percussion, rolling bass and relatively subtle changes. The advertised three-way back-to-back adds the interest of three selectors responding to one another.

HUGEL's night places more recognizable vocals and Latin/Afro rhythmic identity in the foreground. AMÉMÉ, HoneyLuv and MoBlack broaden it beyond one headliner's crossover hits.

For deep dancefloor concentration, favour the opening. For warmth, vocal hooks and an immediately sociable House party, favour HUGEL.

Why the 19 December closing stands out

Prydz supplies patient Progressive House architecture. Mind Against add atmospheric melodic depth. Innellea introduces more restless, cinematic textures. Together, they offer several interpretations of emotionally engaging electronic music across one evening.

The attraction is the breadth of the complete programme. It should reward following the developing mood rather than treating every artist as an isolated headline slot. Individual running times remain unverified.
